## In This Module

- Why a method beats guessing
- Defining the problem before touching anything
- Working the layers in a deliberate order
- What each tool proves, and what it does not
- Changing one thing at a time
- Knowing when to capture packets
- Recording what you found

## Planned Coverage

- Turning a vague report into a testable statement, one machine or many, one service or all
- A layer by layer checklist, link, address, gateway, name resolution, port, application
- The evidence value of each tool, including the common trap of concluding too much from a failed ping
- Failure signatures collected from the earlier modules, timeout versus refused versus reset versus name not resolved
- Isolating variables, and why one change at a time matters
- Reading a capture with a purpose rather than staring at everything
- Recording symptoms, commands, and output so someone else can follow the reasoning

:::tip[Optional Lab]
Break the lab deliberately and come back to it a day later. Work the method and see how far it gets you before you need to guess.
